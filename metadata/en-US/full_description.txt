Calendar is a private calendar for Android. It shows and changes the events kept in the phone’s own calendar storage and has no access to the Internet: syncing is left to the apps that manage your accounts, for example DAVx⁵ with Nextcloud or any other CalDAV server. No account of its own, no ads, no analytics.

Views:
- Month with event tiles that show the start time and wrap long titles, or a compact grid with the chosen day’s list under it
- Week and Day with an all-day strip, overlapping events side by side, a line for the current time and pinch to zoom
- Agenda, a list of the days that have events
- Months scroll as one strip of weeks; a tap on the title goes to any date

Events:
- An event page with location, guests, reminders and description, and your answer to an invitation
- An editor where a new event needs only a title: date and time pickers, Today, Tomorrow and In a week, time zone, availability and visibility
- Repeating events: daily, weekly on chosen days, monthly on a day or on “the third Wednesday”, yearly, ending on a date or after a number of occurrences
- Changing or deleting a repeating event asks once: only this event, this and following events, or all events
- Deleting with Undo; duplicating; sharing as an ICS file or as text

Reminders:
- Posted by the app itself at their exact time, even for events synced while the app was closed
- Snooze, and Directions when the event has a location
- Default reminders for timed and for all-day events

Calendars:
- A color from a palette of twelve and visibility for each calendar, kept by the app
- Calendars that exist only on the phone: create, rename, export to an ICS file, delete
- Import of ICS files opened from another app, shared, or picked: one event or many, with events already saved updated instead of duplicated

And also:
- Search in titles, locations and descriptions, upcoming or past
- First day of the week, first hour of the day, week numbers, the view the app opens with
- Backup and restore of settings and calendar colors to a file you choose
- English and Polish, independent of the system language
- Material 3 Expressive design with your phone’s colors
