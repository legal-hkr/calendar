v0.1.0

First version:
- Month, Week, Day and Agenda views of the calendars already on the phone
- Event page and editor, with repeating events changed one at a time, from one onward, or all at once
- Reminders posted by the app, with Snooze and Directions
- Colors and visibility per calendar, local calendars with export to an ICS file
- Search, import of ICS files, backup and restore of settings
- No Internet access: syncing is left to the apps that manage your accounts
